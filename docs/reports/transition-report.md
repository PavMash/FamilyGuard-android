# Usefulness and Transition Report

#### Is the product complete?  Which parts are done and which aren’t done?

The product is complete and viable, but several bugs remain with Firebase and statistics on child phone usage

#### Is the customer using the product?  How often? In what way? If not, why not?

The customer uses the product only during our meetings due to the problems with Internet connection in his country

#### Has the customer deployed the product on their side?

The customer hopes that he will deploy and use the product in the future, when he will not be busy with his own side-projects

#### What measures need to be taken to fully transition the product?

The customer needs the Android .apk file, code for the server application, and the instructions how to deploy the result product

#### What are the customer’s plans for the product after its delivery?  Are they going to continue working on it? Would they like to continue collaborating with the team and on what conditions?

The customer and his relatives will use the product for some time. If they benefit from it, the customer will continue working on the product. The customer is quite busy, but he is ready to continue collaborating with our team as an advisor

#### How to increase the chance that it’ll be useful after the final delivery?

The customer asked to simplify the dashboard and the Android application for non-technical people

#### Customer feedback on our README:

##### Is everything clear? What can be improved?

The customer confirmed that everything is clear

##### Are they able to launch/deploy using your instructions?

Yes, the instructions are clear and detailed enough. However, the customer advised to make the instructions more verbose for non-technical people (e.g. "How to install Docker")

##### What two other sections the customer would like to be included in the README?

The customer would like to see:

1. The issues of the application being listed in README.md file
2. The things our team learned
3. The obstacles our team has run into

#### Customer feedback and suggestions for improvements

##### MVP v4

Polish the dashboard (simplify), fix bugs

##### MVP v5

Deploy a LLM locally, probably on the phone
